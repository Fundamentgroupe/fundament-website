Research allowance: the plannable building block

The research allowance is a tax incentive under the German Research Allowance Act. It is open to companies of any size and sector, has no call for proposals and can be claimed retroactively. The rate is 35% of eligible costs for small and medium-sized companies and 25% for larger ones. Details are on our page about the research allowance in Germany.

Project funding: grants for specific projects

If a development project is only about to start, grant programmes from the federal government, the states and the EU may be an option. They usually involve selection procedures and deadlines, and are normally applied for before the project begins. Please note: the Central Innovation Programme for SMEs (ZIM) has had a temporary application freeze since July 2026. A restart is aimed for in 2027 but not promised. If you are already developing, the research allowance is the route to look at in the meantime.

We check which programme fits your project, prepare the application documents and look at what can be combined with the research allowance.

Other programmes: energy, digitalisation, investment

Besides research and development, there is funding for energy and resource efficiency, for digitalisation and for investment, partly as grants and partly as low-interest development loans. Programmes, terms and application windows change all the time, so we clarify the current status in a conversation, not on a static page.

Combining programmes without applying twice

Funding programmes follow different rules and deadlines. For the same costs, funding cannot be added up without limit, because cumulation rules apply. We look at all relevant programmes together and decide which costs belong in which programme.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers.

What funding is there for R&D in Germany?

The main building blocks are the research allowance, a tax incentive with a legal entitlement, project funding from the federal government, the states and the EU, and additional programmes for energy efficiency, digitalisation and investment. Which of them fit depends on the type and stage of your project.

When do I have to apply?

That depends on the programme. Project funding usually has to be applied for before the project starts. The research allowance can also be applied for retroactively, up to four years after the end of the calendar year in which the costs were incurred. So it pays to look at planned projects early.

Does the company have to be a certain size or legal form?

No. Size, legal form and sector do not play a decisive role. What matters is that you develop something: new products, processes, software or equipment where the technical outcome is open. If your project is not eligible, we tell you before you hire us.

What does the consulting cost?

The first call and the potential analysis are free, and there is no upfront payment. The fee depends on success and is due only after approval. We discuss the amount in the first call and put it in writing before you sign. We do not guarantee success.
