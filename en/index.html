The research allowance in short

The research allowance is the most predictable grant in the German system. There is no competition and no call for proposals. If the requirements are met, there is a legal entitlement. The rate is 35% of eligible costs for small and medium-sized companies and 25% for larger ones. Research and development starts earlier than many expect: a new control system, a material trial, a formulation or a software architecture with an open outcome. What counts is technical uncertainty, not a laboratory. Routine work such as design to standard, customer adaptations and ongoing quality assurance is not eligible. We tell you before you hire us if your project is unlikely to qualify, and we charge nothing for that.

How we work

  1. First call (15 minutes, free): we discuss your projects and which programmes are realistic.
  2. Analysis (about one week, free): we identify the eligible projects and calculate the expected amount.

  4. Submission and decision: only the company itself can apply. We prepare everything and support you with questions from the authorities.

All questions

What is the research allowance?

The research allowance is a tax-based incentive for research and development under the German Research Allowance Act (FZulG). Companies receive a share of their eligible costs: 35% for small and medium-sized companies and 25% for larger ones. There is no competition and no call for proposals: if the requirements are met, there is a legal entitlement.

Can a foreign-owned company apply?

In principle, any company that is subject to tax in Germany is eligible, regardless of size, legal form and sector. What matters is whether your project meets the criteria for research and development. We clarify this in the first call.

What does it cost?

The intro call and the potential analysis are free, and there is no upfront payment. The fee depends on success and is due only after approval. We discuss the amount in the first call and put it in writing before you sign. We do not guarantee success.
