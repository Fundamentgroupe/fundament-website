Fundament GmbH is based in Münsing, in Bavaria near Munich. We advise companies on grants for research, development and innovation in Germany. The research allowance is one important topic, but it is not the only one. We also look at project funding from federal, state and EU programmes, and at programmes for energy efficiency, digitalisation and investment.

The fastest way: a 15-minute intro call

Most people start with a short call. You choose a time that suits you in our online calendar. Booking runs through Calendly. When you click the button, you leave this site. Details are in our privacy notice, which is available in German.

Book a free intro call

What happens in the call

You do not need to prepare any documents. It is enough if you briefly describe what you are working on: a new product, a process, software or a plant, and where the project stands today. We then sort out which grant programmes are realistic for your project and roughly how large the funding could be.

We say openly if a grant does not apply to your project. If it does, we explain the next steps. A free analysis follows, in which we separate the eligible projects and calculate the expected amount. You can see the full process on our page How it works.

What it costs

The intro call and the first analysis are free. There is no upfront payment. Our fee depends on success and becomes due only after your grant is approved. We discuss the amount in the intro call and put it in writing before you sign anything.

We do not give a guarantee of success. Whether a project is eligible is decided by the certification body and the tax office, not by us.

Prefer to write or call?

You can reach us by email at kontakt@fundamentgroup.de or by phone at +49 170 2216826. In your first message, a few lines are enough: your company, the project, and the question you have. We will reply with a suggestion for how to proceed.

If you would like to check first on your own, try the funding check (in German). It helps you see which programmes may be relevant.

Who we work with

We work with companies that develop something: new products, processes, software or plants where the technical result is not certain in advance. Size, legal form and industry do not play a decisive role. If you are based in Germany or plan a project here, you are welcome to get in touch.
