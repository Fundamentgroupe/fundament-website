Five steps from first call to payout

Fundament is a grant consulting firm in Münsing, Bavaria. We help companies in Germany find the funding that fits their development work and guide them through the application. Research allowance is one important topic, alongside project funding and state programmes. This is how a typical project runs.

  1. Intro call (15 minutes, free): We assess which programmes are realistic for your project.
  2. Analysis (about one week, free): We define which projects can qualify for funding and calculate the expected amount.
  3. Preparation (two to four weeks): We talk with your developers and prepare project descriptions plus cost and time records.
  4. Submission and decision: Only the company itself is entitled to apply. We prepare everything fully and support you with follow-up questions.
  5. Payout and following years: We set up the documentation so that the next cycle runs faster.

What we need from you

For the research allowance, our experience is five to eight hours of your time per application year. That is mainly two conversations with the developers involved, cost data, and your approval. We write the project descriptions and prepare the supporting records. With a large development team or many parallel projects, the effort on your side can be higher. We tell you how much after the analysis, not before.

Who submits the application

By law, only the company can apply. The application is usually submitted through your own access or through your tax advisor. The research allowance is requested from the certification body (Bescheinigungsstelle Forschungszulage) first and then from the tax office. We prepare the application and support you with questions from the certification body and the tax office.

Combining programmes

Funding programmes follow different rules and deadlines. For the same costs, funding cannot be added up without limit, because cumulation rules apply. We look at all programmes that could fit together and decide which costs belong in which programme.

Project funding usually has to be applied for before the project starts. The research allowance can also be requested for past years, up to four years after the end of the calendar year in which the costs arose.

Costs and what we do not promise

The intro call and the potential analysis are free, and there is no upfront payment. Our fee depends on success and becomes due only after approval. See our success fee model for details. We do not give a success guarantee: the certification body and the tax office decide whether a project is eligible. If your project is not eligible, we tell you before you hire us.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers.

How long does the process take?

The analysis takes about one week and preparation two to four weeks. The certification body usually needs about three months to process the application, and its application is free of charge.

How much work is it for our team?

In our experience five to eight hours per application year for the research allowance: mainly two conversations with the developers involved, cost data and your approval. We write the project descriptions and records.

Can we apply for past years?

In many cases yes. The research allowance can be requested up to four years after the end of the calendar year in which the costs arose. Project funding usually has to be applied for before the project starts.

Who submits the application?

Only the company is entitled to apply by law. The application is usually submitted through your own access or your tax advisor. We prepare it fully and support you with follow-up questions.
